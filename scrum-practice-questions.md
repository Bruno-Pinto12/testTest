# Scrum Practice Questions

Numbered by uploaded image, not by the original exam question number.
Answers are the correct choices, not necessarily the choices selected in the images.

## Image 1 — Scrum framework

**Question:** The Scrum framework is purposefully incomplete.

**Possible answers:**
- A. False
- B. True

**Correct answer:** B. True

**Exam explanation:** Scrum defines essential rules, not every implementation technique.

---

## Image 2 — Product Backlog refinement

**Question:** When should Product Backlog refinement occur?

**Possible answers:**
- A. Continuously throughout the Sprint
- B. Only during Sprint Planning
- C. Only during the Sprint Review
- D. At the start of each Sprint

**Correct answer:** A. Continuously throughout the Sprint

**Exam explanation:** Refinement is ongoing, as needed—not necessarily nonstop.

---

## Image 3 — Choosing a Scrum Master

**Question:** Who may be best positioned to decide who will be the Scrum Master for a new Scrum Team?

**Possible answers:**
- A. The Product Owner
- B. Stakeholders
- C. The Developers and the Product Owner
- D. The Developers

**Best answer:** C. The Developers and the Product Owner

**Exam explanation:** They understand the team's needs. Scrum does not prescribe an appointment procedure.

## Image 4 — Product Owner at the Retrospective

**Question:** The Product Owner must attend the Sprint Retrospective

**Possible answers:**
- A. False
- B. True

**Correct answer:** B. True

**Exam explanation:** The Retrospective involves the entire Scrum Team, including the Product Owner.

## Image 5 — Sprint Planning topics

**Question:** Which of the following best describes the topics discussed at Sprint Planning?

**Possible answers:**
- A. Who is on the team and what team member roles will be.
- B. How conditions have changed and how the Product Backlog should evolve.
- C. What to do and who will do it.
- D. What went wrong in the last Sprint and what to do differently this Sprint.
- E. Why the work is valuable, what can be done, and how to do it.

**Correct answer:** E. Why the work is valuable, what can be done, and how to do it.

**Exam explanation:** Remember: Why, What, How.

## Image 6 — Timeboxing

**Question:** Which of the following is a benefit of working inside timeboxes?

**Possible answers:**
- A. It ensures that all planned items will be finished.
- B. It helps the Product Owner know which attribute of the "Iron Triangle" to adjust.
- C. It increases the Scrum Team's velocity.
- D. It helps to limit work-in-progress.

**Best answer:** D. It helps to limit work-in-progress.

**Exam explanation:** Timeboxes encourage bounded focus; completion and increased velocity are not guaranteed.

## Image 7 — Technical debt

**Question:** A Scrum Team must never have any technical debt.

**Possible answers:**
- A. False
- B. True

**Correct answer:** A. False

**Exam explanation:** Zero technical debt is not a Scrum requirement; quality must not decrease.

## Image 8 — Product Goal

**Question:** A Scrum Team must only work on a single Product Goal at any time

**Possible answers:**
- A. False
- B. True

**Correct answer:** B. True

**Exam explanation:** Fulfill or abandon the current Product Goal before adopting another.

## Image 9 — Missing testing expertise

**Question:** Among the Developers, there is nobody with significant testing expertise. They should...

**Instruction:** Select 2 answers.

**Possible answers:**
- A. Quality is the responsibility of the Developers and they should undertake testing themselves to the best of their abilities
- B. Produce an Increment that will be tested by a dedicated test team after the Sprint to guarantee the quality
- C. Request for a specialist tester to join them and queue testing for them to do when they arrive in a later Sprint
- D. Raise this as an impediment which may require the assistance of the Scrum Master to resolve

**Correct answers:** A and D.

**Exam explanation:** Developers retain quality accountability. Missing skills are an impediment; testing cannot be deferred to declare work Done.

## Image 10 — Sprint length

**Question:** What factor should be considered when establishing the Sprint length? (choose the best answer)

**Possible answers:**
- A. The need for the team to learn based on doing work and measuring results.
- B. The organization's release schedule.
- C. The frequency at which team formation can be changed.
- D. The organization has mandated similar length sprints.

**Correct answer:** A. The need for the team to learn based on doing work and measuring results.

**Exam explanation:** Choose learning and risk control, not release scheduling.

## Image 13 — Product Owner and Scrum Master membership

**Question:** All Scrum Teams will have a Scrum Master and Product Owner.

**Possible answers:**
- A. True. The Product Owner and Scrum Master must be a full time member to the Scrum Team.
- B. True. Outcomes are affected by their level of participation and availability.
- C. False. A Business Analyst can take on the same responsibilities of a Product Owner.
- D. False. A Scrum Master is only needed if or when requested by the Developers.

**Correct answer:** B. True. Outcomes are affected by their level of participation and availability.

**Exam explanation:** Both accountabilities are required; full-time dedication is not prescribed.

## Image 14 — Ordered versus prioritised

**Question:** Why does Scrum require the Product Backlog to be ordered rather than prioritised?

**Possible answers:**
- A. Order and priority are always the same thing
- B. Multiple PBI's can have the same priority. There can only be one position in the Product Backlog when it comes to order.
- C. Order is better than chaos
- D. Scrum requires both order and priority

**Best answer:** B. Multiple PBI's can have the same priority. There can only be one position in the Product Backlog when it comes to order.

**Exam explanation:** Ordering gives a sequence, not merely shared priority labels.

## Image 15 — Shortest listed timebox

**Question:** Which of the following events has the shortest timebox?

**Possible answers visible in the image:**
- A. Sprint Planning
- B. Sprint Retrospective
- C. Sprint Review

**Correct answer:** B. Sprint Retrospective

**Exam explanation:** One-month Sprint maxima: Planning 8 hours, Review 4, Retrospective 3.

**Exam trap:** Answer among the listed options. The Daily Scrum is 15 minutes but is not listed.

## Image 17 — Product Backlog completeness

**Question:** When is the Product Backlog considered complete?

**Possible answers:**
- A. When all items in it have been completed
- B. At the end of each Sprint
- C. When the product is released
- D. It is unlikely to ever be truly complete

**Correct answer:** D. It is unlikely to ever be truly complete

## Image 19 — Daily Scrum

**Question:** The Scrum Master must facilitate the Daily Scrum

**Possible answers:**
- False
- True

**Correct answer:** False

**Exam explanation:** The Daily Scrum is for the Developers. The Scrum Master may help, but is not required to facilitate it.

## Image 20 — Cross-functional team

**Question:** Which statement best describes a cross-functional team?

**Possible answers:**
- All members of the team have all the skills required to create the Product
- The team has a good mix of skills
- The team has all competencies needed to accomplish the work

**Correct answer:** The team has all competencies needed to accomplish the work

**Exam explanation:** Cross-functional means the team as a whole has the skills needed to create value each Sprint; not every person needs every skill.
