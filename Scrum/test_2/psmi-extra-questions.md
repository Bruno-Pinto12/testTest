# PSM I Extra Questions (from screenshots)

## Image 74 — Release Sprint

**Question:** True or False: A high-performance Scrum Team ensures that each Increment is complete by running a Release Sprint.

**Possible answers:**
- True
- False

**Correct answer:** False

**Exam explanation:** Scrum does not define a "Release Sprint." Each Sprint should produce a usable Increment that meets the Definition of Done.

## Image 75 — Scrum Values and trust

**Question:** Which Scrum Value is affected by a lack of trust in the Scrum Team? (choose the best answer)

**Possible answers:**
- A. Focus
- B. Respect
- C. Openness
- D. Courage
- E. Commitment
- F. All of the above

**Correct answer:** F. All of the above

**Exam explanation:** Lack of trust impacts all Scrum Values, not just one.

## Image 76 — Product Backlog ordering and value flow

**Question:** The Developers ask their Product Owner to re-order the Product Backlog. The team is waiting for an external supplier to deliver a component. Without that component there will not be enough work in the next Sprint to occupy the full team. As the Scrum Master, what advice would you give the Product Owner? (choose the best answer)

**Possible answers:**
- A. Tell the Product Owner to re-order the Product Backlog so the work involving the external component can be planned in a separate sprint.
- B. Tell the Product Owner that the Product Backlog should be ordered to maximize utilization of the Developers.
- C. Remind the Product Owner that their primary concern is the flow of value reflected in the ordering of the Product Backlog.

**Correct answer:** C. Remind the Product Owner that their primary concern is the flow of value reflected in the ordering of the Product Backlog.

**Exam explanation:** Product Backlog ordering should optimize value, not local utilization.

## Image 77 — Forming multiple Scrum Teams

**Question:** In accordance with Scrum theory, how should a group of 100 people be divided into multiple Scrum Teams? (choose the best answer)

**Possible answers:**
- A. It does not really matter since you can rotate the teams every Sprint to spread knowledge.
- B. Create a matrix of skills, seniority, and level of experience to assign people to teams.
- C. Check with the allocation department to see who has worked together before and make these the first teams.
- D. Understanding the product, the product vision and the rules of the Scrum framework, the people divide themselves into teams.

**Correct answer:** D. Understanding the product, the product vision and the rules of the Scrum framework, the people divide themselves into teams.

**Exam explanation:** Scrum favors self-managing teams that organize themselves around delivering value.
