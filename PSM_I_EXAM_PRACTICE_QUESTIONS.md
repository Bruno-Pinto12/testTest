## Question 1   🔁 Seen 4 times (3 duplicates)

**True or False: It is mandatory that the product Increment be released to production at the end of each Sprint.**

A) True

B) False

**Answer:** B

**Key note:** A usable Increment may be released before the Sprint ends; Scrum does not require a release at every Sprint boundary.
